# Words for what looks wrong

For when something's off and the word won't come. Skim the left column; the
agent uses the same words when it offers choices. "Looks like" is what you
see, "usual fix" is what the agent will try.

| Word | Looks like | Usual fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Silhouette** | Filled in black, it doesn't read as the thing; a blob | Exaggerate the one or two shapes that name it; cut gaps between parts |
| **Proportion** | A part is the wrong size next to its neighbours | Scale that part; check it against the sketch in a flat view |
| **Chunkiness** | Too thin and spindly, or too thick and lumpy, for our cartoony style | Thicken or thin the main members together |
| **Busy / noisy** | Too many small bits; the eye has nowhere to rest | Remove or merge small parts; move detail into paint |
| **Flat / plain** | Nothing to look at; big empty areas | Add one feature or a paint break; vary the shape (bevel, taper) |
| **Contrast** | Parts blend into each other; colours too close | Push one part lighter or darker, or change its hue |
| **Readability** | Fine up close, mush from the game camera | Bigger, fewer shapes; stronger colour breaks |
| **Weight / balance** | Looks like it would tip over, or feels top-heavy | Widen the base, lower the mass, or shift the big part |
| **Tangent edges** | Two outlines just touch, so they merge into one line | Overlap them clearly or separate them clearly |
| **Floating** | A part hovers, not attached | Seat it into its neighbour, or add the joint holding it |
| **Clipping** | One part pokes through another | Move it out, or shape it round the other |
| **Stiff** | Everything straight and square; no life | Tilt, taper, bend or dent some parts (`imperfect`) |
| **Style drift** | Doesn't look like it belongs with our other models | Compare against a model you like; match its thickness, colours and wear |
| **Scale** | Wrong size against the player (4×5.5 capsule) | Resize the whole; recheck clearances |

You can also just point: say a cell from the gridded screenshot ("C4"), or
pick one of the A/B/C versions the agent shows. "Closer to B, but more" is a
perfectly good answer.
