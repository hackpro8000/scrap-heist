# Map Theme: City Scrapyard

The map is a scrapyard built into a ruined city block. Nothing grows here. Every
tree, rock, bush and patch of grass is made from city junk arranged into the
shape of the natural thing it stands in for. From a distance it should read as a
park or a hillside. Up close, it should read as scrap.

## Rules
- Each prop keeps the silhouette of the thing it replaces. A tree is still tall
  with a crown, and a rock is still a low, heavy lump.
- Build from recognisable city junk: hubcaps, pipes, signs, drums, tyres, car
  panels, rebar and wire.
- Props sit on z 0, are centered on the origin, and use 1 unit = 1 stud.

## Palette
- Rust: `RUST_*`
- Concrete: grey (0.6, 0.59, 0.56)
- Hazard yellow: (0.9, 0.72, 0.1)
- Copper: `COPPER`
- Steel: `STEEL` and `DARK_STEEL`
- Rubber: `RUBBER_BLACK`
